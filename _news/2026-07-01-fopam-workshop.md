We are organizing a **1.5-day pre-conference workshop** at FOPAM 2026 (Foundations of Process/Product Analytics and Machine Learning) in Decatur, GA on **July 26–27, 2026**.

**Foundations to Frontier: Machine Learning & AI for the Process Industries**

This workshop offers a single, coherent arc through ML and AI for the process industries — from classical statistical foundations to deep learning, LLMs, and agents, to what actually works on the plant floor.

**Three modules taught by leaders spanning academia, pharma R&D, and chemicals manufacturing:**

1. **Design of Experiments, Data Exploration, Classical Regression & Active Learning** — Jose E. Tabora (Bristol Myers Squibb)
2. **Modern ML/AI: Deep Learning, LLMs, and Agents** — Bhushan Gopaluni (UBC)
3. **AI/ML in Industry: A Journey Through Industrial Examples** — Bea Braun (Dow)

See the workshop page for the full schedule, module details, and registration: [/fopam2026/]({{ site.baseurl }}/fopam2026/).
