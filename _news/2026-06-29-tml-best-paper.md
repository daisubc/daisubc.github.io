Congratulations to DAIS Lab PhD student **Liang Cao** and co-authors for winning the **Best Paper Award** at the International Workshop on Trustworthy Multimodal Learning for Social Media Analysis (TML 2026), held at The Web Conference 2026 in Dubai, UAE.

**Paper:** *Sparse Causal Latent Features for Robust Multimodal Learning under Distribution Shifts*

**Authors:** Liang Cao, Weide Liu, Yan Qin, Zhenghua Chen, Zhuo Chen, Bhushan Gopaluni

This work addresses the challenging problem of maintaining robust performance in multimodal learning systems when facing distribution shifts — a critical issue for deploying AI systems in real-world applications.
