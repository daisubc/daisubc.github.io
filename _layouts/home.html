Research Themes

We develop AI and machine learning methods for industrial process control — from theory and algorithms to deployment with industry partners.

Intelligent Control

Deep reinforcement learning, meta-RL, and model predictive control that adapt to changing process conditions without manual retuning.

Digital Twins & Modeling

Large-scale dynamic models and hybrid physics-ML models for simulation, optimization, and control of complex industrial processes.

Fault Detection & Diagnostics

Anomaly detection, root cause analysis, and interpretable ML for diagnosing faults in complex industrial systems.

Predictive Analytics

Soft sensors, forecasting models, and visualization tools for reliable predictions from large-scale industrial data.
